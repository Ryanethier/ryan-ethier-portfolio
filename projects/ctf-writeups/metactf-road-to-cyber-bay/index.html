MetaCTF: Road to Cyber Bay

Team competition covering reverse engineering, binary exploitation, and forensics/data analysis challenges. Our team, LinuxCats333, placed 34th out of 400+ teams and qualified for the invite-only championship event in Tampa. Below are detailed write-ups for each challenge we solved.

01 BitOnion

A layered, reversible bit-manipulation cipher applied to a flag file. Reverse engineering the binary reveals the operation log appended after each pass, letting us peel the transformations back in reverse.

Reverse Engineering 207 pts

02 DocScan

A document-scanning utility with a stack buffer overflow that cascades into a format string vulnerability, enabling a write-what-where primitive and a GOT overwrite to pop a shell.

Binary Exploitation 211 pts

03 Election Fraud Detection

Statistical analysis of a fictional state's voting records to detect machine tampering by comparing presidential vote rates against down-ballot consistency at each station.

Forensics / Data Analysis 186 pts

04 TinyPacked

A custom MessagePack extension format using dictionary-key deduplication and delta-encoded integer arrays. Writing a custom unpacker reconstructs the original structure and the flag.
